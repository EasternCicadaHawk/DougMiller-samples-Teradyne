Supported SFP and SFP+ Connectors

When looking at SFP+ ports on the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance, the interface's left receptacle is the receive connection and the interface's right receptacle is the transmit connection. The SFP+ ports accept these connector types:

10GBASE-SR and 1000BASE-SX (short wavelength) use a multi-mode LC connector.

10GBASE-LR and 1000BASE-LX (long wavelength) use either a multi-mode or single-mode LC connector.

1000BASE-T uses an RJ-45 connector.

A number of SFP and SFP+ modules have been tested with the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance. If you intend to purchase SFP/SFP+ modules from a third-party supplier, select from the lists below.

Note: Configurations that use untested SFP/SFP+ modules are not supported.
