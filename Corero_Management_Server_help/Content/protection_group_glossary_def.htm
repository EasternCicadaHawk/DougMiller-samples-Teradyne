Protection Group

A protection group is the unit of HA redundancy in the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance. A protection group is associated with a cluster. If the appliances in the cluster operate individually, the cluster comprises a single protection group only. However, if the appliances in the cluster are configured for HA redundancy, then there will be two protection groups; if an appliance in one protection group fails, the second protection group will take over the handling of traffic.
