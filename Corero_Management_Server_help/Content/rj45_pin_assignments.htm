RJ-45 Connector Pin Assignments

The 10BASE-T/100BASE-TX/1000BASE-T copper LAN ports on the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance use RJ-45 connectors.

The pin assignments for these connectors follow:

Pin Assignment
1 Rx1+
2 Rx1-
3 Tx1+
4 No connection
5 No connection
6 Tx1-
7 No connection
8 No connection
