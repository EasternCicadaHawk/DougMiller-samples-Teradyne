Upgrading multiple Corero appliances causes the CMS to restart

When attempting a multi-appliance upgrade, the CMS will restart. Prior to the restart, though, the correct signals are sent to the appliances, and the image that is pushed does in fact activate, and the upgrades succeed on the appliances. However, the CMS will subsequently get a java.net.ConnectException: Connection refused and the CMS will restart.
