Device

A device is any purpose-built security appliance provided by Corero Network Security. A device can be a physical hardware unit, such as a appliance, or it can be a software-only virtual appliance, such as a vDDP. Functionally, the two forms are similar, and each qualifies as a device for the management purposes of the CMS.
