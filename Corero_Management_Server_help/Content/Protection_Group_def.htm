Protection Group

A protection group is a collection of appliances that you want to have use the same security settings. A protection group typically comprises appliances that share a common role or location at your site. The purpose of a protection group is to allow you to create and modify policies and environmental configuration items and apply them as a group of settings to one or more appliances. You can also modify the settings within a protection group by automatically updating certain environmental configuration items using Threat Update Service Defense Packs.

Note: Protection groups were known as "policy groups" in previous releases of Corero software.
