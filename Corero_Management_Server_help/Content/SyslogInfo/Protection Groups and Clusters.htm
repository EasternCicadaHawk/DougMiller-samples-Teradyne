Protection Groups and Clusters

A protection group is the unit of protection and security services unit for a single 10Gbps link. A cluster comprises one or two protection groups, depending on whether the cluster is configured for HA redundancy or not.

The status and statistics for a cluster and protection group are indicated in the syslog message with the cl and pg keywords.   Every 60 seconds the status and statics for all protection groups are sent in syslog messages.  Additionally, the status and statistics for the site as the aggregate of all of the protection groups are sent.  In these messages, the cl and pg keywords contain the value of “all”.
