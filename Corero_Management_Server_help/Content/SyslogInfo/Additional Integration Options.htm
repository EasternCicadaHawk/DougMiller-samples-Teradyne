Additional Integration Options

Corero can help partners deliver a leadership DDoS managed service through tighter integration between Corero’s DDS and the partners’ management systems and analysis tools.  Such integration can allow partners access to the complete set of security information available in the DDS.  Partners can contact Corero to investigate the available options by completing the partner inquiry form at http://www.corero.com/en/partners/become_a_partner or via e-mail at partners@corero.com.

About Corero

Corero Network Security (CNS:LN) is the global provider of the “First Line of Defense”. Enterprises rely on Corero to protect their critical online assets from the risks associated with network-borne cyber-threats. Corero is headquartered in Massachusetts, U.S. with offices worldwide.
