Volumetric Attack

A volumetric attack is one that seeks to overwhelm its target by consuming all of one or more of its available resources, such as bandwidth or memory. In a volumetric attack, the attacker directs so much illegitimate traffic at the target that traffic from legitimate users is starved of the targeted resource.and denied altogether. TCP SYN floods, ICMP floods, and UDP floods are common examples of volumetric attacks.
