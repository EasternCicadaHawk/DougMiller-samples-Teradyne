Reflection Attack

A reflection attack uses a spoofed IP address to generate attack traffic, with the false IP address being that of the target.

Reflection attacks often are UDP-based. UDP's connectionless design, its trust of the IP addresses it sees in UDP packets, and the ability of many UDP-based protocols to respond with messages much larger than the corresponding requests make it easy to exploit for DDoS attacks.

Some reflection attacks, when used in conjunction with a protocol capable of replying with a larger amount of traffic than that used to request a response, are amplification attacks.
