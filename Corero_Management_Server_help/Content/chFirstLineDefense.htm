Corero Product Overview: The First Line of Defense

Corero Network Security provides the solution to a common problem that often impairs the performance and effectiveness of many organizations' IT infrastructures.

Recognizing a Common IT Infrastructure Problem

Today, an organization’s network can be overwhelmed by DDoS attacks and cyber threats coming in from the Internet. This can comprise a number of hazards, from undesired users and services on your network to distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks and advanced evasions that target your servers. Unfortunately, today’s security infrastructure is not sufficient to stop these encroachments on your network. Specifically:

  • The state tables of current generation firewalls get saturated with large numbers of connections from unwanted traffic.
  • Next generation firewalls don’t have adequate DDoS protection, and therefore cannot discriminate good traffic from bad traffic.
  • IT infrastructure and servers sitting downstream from the firewall end up processing unnecessary traffic and therefore are less responsive to good traffic.

This not only exposes you to threats, but costs service degradation, site downtime, brand damage, and, most importantly, lost business.

The Solution: Corero’s First Line of Defense

The solution to this problem, Corero’s First Line of Defense®, stops DDoS attacks, protects infrastructure, and eliminates downtime from its position in front of your firewall. The First Line of Defense stops all types of DDoS attacks, targeted server attacks, advanced evasions and a host of other attack vectors including mitigation of DDoS attacks and cyber threats before they even enter your network. As a result, it makes your IT infrastructure more efficient and effective, improving performance of applications and servers, and ultimately eliminating downtime. More importantly, the assumption you had in the beginning, that your customer traffic flows through unabated, now actually holds true. You have satisfied customers and foiled attackers.

Corero’s First Line of Defense mitigates DDoS attacks and cyber threats with a systematic approach comprising five steps:

1. Allow expected traffic while restricting known attackers. Examples of known attackers are IP addresses known to be malicious and geographies that you don’t do business with. Corero’s ReputationWatch® filters them out ahead of time so that no traffic from these sources comes in.
2. Thwart attackers based on their behavior. Examples include an attacker who throws large volumes of traffic at your website, or who opens an excessive number of connections, tying up your server resources. Corero’s dynamic threat assessment and DDoS rejection capabilities mitigate these hazards by evaluating their behavior and limiting their rate, or blocking them altogether.
3. Enforce correct traffic behavior by checking whether traffic conforms to RFC specifications or de facto standards. For example, normal behavior does not send payload traffic in tiny fragments. Corero’s protocol validation modules (PVMs) stop such protocol abuse and advanced evasion techniques (AETs).
4. Analyze traffic integrity by looking at whether it contains any known intrusions. Corero uses signatures to inspect traffic and block any buffer overflows, code injections, and exploits before they compromise your servers. With Protection Packs, key components of Corero’s threat update service, Corero keeps its customers up to date with the latest security protection by delivering malware signatures and vulnerability protection on a weekly basis.
5. Provide you with insight into what is happening on your network. This includes the ability to collate data from SYSLOG events, SNMP gets & traps, and proprietary APIs into a meaningful stream of information. With that, you can reassess the threat landscape to see if your security policies are effective or need modifications. Corero’s SecureWatch®, a maintenance and monitoring service, provides infrastructure and expertise to keep Corero deployments up to date and assure sustained availability.

Corero supports this approach with a product that acts as the First Line of Defense for your network: the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance (referred to simply as an "appliance"). This device is managed using the Corero Management Server, an application designed expressly for configuring, managing, and monitoring the appliances that protect your network from harm.
