Site

A site is the set of appliances and policies that are managed by a single instance of the CMS.

Although the appliances and the servers that they protect should reside in the same location, the definition of a site, for the purposes of the appliance and the CMS, is not a geographical term. Also, while the CMS manages the protection of servers within your organization's firewall, the CMS does not manage the servers themselves; the servers can be associated with the site using elements such as server groups, but the constituent servers are not among the devices managed by the CMS.
