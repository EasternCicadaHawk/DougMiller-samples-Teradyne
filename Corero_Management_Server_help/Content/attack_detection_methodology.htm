Attack Detection Methodology

Practice the following methodology for identifying and mitigating attacks on your network:

  1. Research. Peruse dashboard data and performance statistics for your network regularly, and develop an understanding of what constitutes normal or typical behavior for your network. Noticing aberrant behavior is the first step in recognizing an attack.
  2. Protect. Define security policies that guard your network against unwanted or dangerous traffic. This will stop many attacks before they have any chance to impair your network's performance.
  3. Detect. Use dashboard data and performance statistics for your network to recognize aberrant traffic that may indicate an attack. Unusual increases in certain types of traffic or unusual reductions in network performance may well indicate an attack.
  4. Triage. Examine unusual dashboard data and performance statistics to assess the attack. How does the attack manifest? What is its apparent target? What is its apparent source?
  5. Mitigate. Take immediate action to reduce the effectiveness of the attack. This may mean shunning one or more client IP addresses, disabling access for a particular service, or changing a limit on the number of connections or number of packets that are allowed for a service. Even if you cannot stop the attack completely without also inhibiting desired traffic, you can reduce the attack's ability to impair your network's proper function.
  6. Rectify. Adjust your security policies to prevent the attack from impairing your network performance again.
