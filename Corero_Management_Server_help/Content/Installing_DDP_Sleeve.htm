Installing the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance Rack-Mount Sleeve

A sheetmetal rack-mount sleeve is provided with the SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliance to facilitate the addition and removal of SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliances in the rack. The sleeve is mounted in the rack and fastened to it, and SmartWall Network Threat Defense Appliances are simply inserted in the four slots in the sleeve, latching in place.
