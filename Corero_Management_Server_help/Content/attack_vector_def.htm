Attack Vector

An attack vector is the medium by which an attack is delivered to its target. Attack vectors include compromised client computers and botnets of compromised clients, compromised servers, .malware-bearing web pages, phishing Email messages, and viruses and assorted malicious Email attachments, to name a few of the more common ones. Realistically, the set of possible attack vectors is limited only by attackers' imagination and ingenuity.

Increasingly, as techniques for defending infrastructure against attacks become more sophisticated, attackers are responding by developing multi-vector attacks. Multi-vector attacks combine two or more attack vectors in an effort to circumvent a target's defenses more rapidly and effectively.
