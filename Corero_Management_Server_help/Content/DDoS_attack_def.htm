DDoS Attack

A distributed denial of service (DDoS) attack is an event in which multiple sources assault a destination server with a volume of traffic that overwhelms that server's ability to respond to any traffic at all, making it unavailable to the legitimate traffic for which it is intended.

DDoS attacks can take many different forms and exploit a wide variety of protocol behaviors and design features to achieve their goal of making a server unavailable.
